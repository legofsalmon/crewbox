# Files, filtering and search

A festival channel fills up fast — stage plots, photos of a broken casing,
fifty messages about lunch. Crewbox gives you three tools: share files
properly, filter what's in front of you, and search everything ever said.

## Sharing files and photos

Three ways in:

1. The **paperclip** in the composer — pick a file or take a photo.
2. **Drag and drop** anywhere onto the conversation — the overlay says
   "Drop to share". Dropping several uploads them all.
3. **Paste** — a screenshot on the clipboard pastes straight into the
   composer.

Images appear as previews in the conversation; other files as a card with
name and size. Tap one for the detail view: open, download, copy a link,
share it on (on phones), or delete it. You can always delete your own
uploads; deleting anyone else's needs admin. Photos zoom with a pinch or a
double-tap.

Files live on the box, not on anyone's phone — so an attachment posted at
noon is still there for the night crew, whoever's phone took it.

## The filter bar

The **funnel** button in the channel header narrows what's in front of you:

- Chips: **All · Photos · Files · Links · Mentions** — one tap to see just
  the photos in `#stage`.
- A **person picker** listing everyone who has actually spoken in this
  channel — combine with a chip: "photos from Dev".

The bar counts what it's looking through — "34 of 210 loaded" — because it
**only filters messages already loaded on this device**. Tap **Load older**
to widen it. An empty result means "nothing in what's loaded", not "nothing
ever" — for the whole history, use search.

The filter clears itself when you switch channels or close the bar, so a
forgotten filter can never make a channel look silently empty.

## Search

The **magnifier** in the header — or **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** anywhere — opens
search. It asks the box, not your device, so it covers **the whole history
of every channel you can see**, whether or not it's loaded on your phone.
Results show author, channel and date; tapping one jumps to that message in
place, highlighted.

## Which tool when

| You want                                        | Use                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| The photos someone posted this afternoon        | Filter bar, **Photos** chip                           |
| Everything one person said here today           | Filter bar, person picker                             |
| "Where did anyone mention the generator, ever?" | **Search** (⌘K)                                       |
| The stage plot PDF from three days ago          | **Search**, or the **Files** chip after loading older |
