# Chat

Chat is the spine of crewbox: channels for departments, DMs for everything
else, and alerts that reach a phone in a pocket. It's always on, whatever
modules the box runs.

## Channels

Channels are open rooms — every crew member can read and join any of them.
The box starts with `#general`; anyone can add more with the **+** next to
"Channels" (names come out lowercase, spaces become hyphens: "Stage 2" →
`#stage-2`). Make channels for departments or stages, not for moments — a
festival needs `#stage`, `#foh`, `#site`, not `#friday-issue-3`.

Unread messages show as a count on the channel row; if someone mentioned
you, the badge changes to an `@` style so you can see at a glance which
unreads actually need you.

## Direct messages

Every crew member on the box is listed under **Direct messages** — online
people first, each with a presence dot. A DM is private between the two of
you. An `office` tag beside a name means they're connected from off the
event network (from the production office over a tunnel, say) — useful to
know before you ask them to physically go and look at something.

## Mentions

Type `@` and pick a name — `@Maya` lights the message up for Maya and makes
her unread badge urgent. `@all`, `@everyone` and `@channel` flag everyone in
the channel; use them like a PA announcement, sparingly. Your own mentions
are highlighted more strongly, so skimming a busy channel for "things aimed
at me" is fast.

## Alerts

Mentions and DMs chirp and vibrate; the bell button in the sidebar mutes the
sounds when you're next to the stage. If the app is in the background, you
get a notification. For alerts that reach a **locked phone with no
internet**, use the Android app — the **Phones and platforms** page covers
what each platform can and can't do.

## Writing messages

- **Enter** sends on a computer; on a phone, Enter makes a new line and the
  send button sends.
- A half-typed message survives switching channels — it's waiting when you
  come back. (It doesn't survive reloading the app.)
- Links become clickable automatically. A crewbox link someone shares — a
  patch sheet, a plot, the network audit — appears as an **Open ↗** chip
  that jumps straight there.
- "X is typing…" under the messages means an answer may already be on its
  way.

## What "sending…" means

A message marked **sending…** is safely stored on your device and will be
delivered — once, in order — when the box is reachable. It is not lost, and
resending it by hand will only make a duplicate. This is the offline-first
design doing its job; there's more in
[Around the app](/docs/around-the-app#offline-is-normal-here).
